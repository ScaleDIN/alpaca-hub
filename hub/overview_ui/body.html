All devices

Every connected device on one time axis: 1 s detail for the last 10 minutes, 10-second averages before that (1-minute ones from the day files); the band around a line is the lowest to highest reading, so a short spike still shows (drawn when one or two values are on; Range switches it on or off for every view). Before what the devices hold in memory, the chart reads their day files below, back 7 days: a time the hub or PC was off shows as a gap. Values with the same unit share a scale.
