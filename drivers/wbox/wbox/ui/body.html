Power

Regulation

The automatic modes (constant temperature, dew point offset) run on this PC: the stock firmware only knows a fixed heater power. Changes apply at once. These settings aren't part of the Alpaca switches, so Alpaca programs can't change them and they aren't locked.

Temperature probes

A probe that isn't used shows as "not used" instead of MISSING, its Alpaca gauges are left out, and without the DHT22 the ObservingConditions device reports no temperature, humidity or dew point. The dew modes that need it can't be picked. Applies at once; reconnect the Switch in the Alpaca program to see the change.

ASCOM Alpaca

Devices

Alpaca clients find this service on their own (discovery on UDP 32227). It offers:

Switch #0USB, DC2 (on/off and voltage), DC4-6, dew heater (mode, manual power, target, offset), and the gauges chosen below
ObservingConditions #0Temperature, humidity and dew point from the box's DHT22

The two devices are named after it: " outputs" and " sensors". Their unique IDs don't change. Alpaca programs show the new name the next time they look for devices or connect.

Gauges (Switch ids 8 and up)

Read-only values on the Alpaca Switch device, for any Alpaca program (usually in its Switch panel). Reconnect the Switch in the program to see the change. While a probe is missing, its gauges are named e.g. "Lens temperature: MISSING" (NINA, for one, updates names as it polls). A fixed value is kept inside each gauge's range. The writable switches (ids 0-7) never move; the gauges follow from id 8 in the order above.

WSM firmware

Both are stored in the box, so they apply whatever program switches DC2. The ceiling is a hard limit: set it to the rating of the camera on DC2 (8.4 V for a dummy battery, for example); a lower ceiling also lowers a setpoint above it. A slow ramp keeps the inrush into DC2's load small; 5 V/s reaches 12 V in 2.4 s, 20 V/s in 0.6 s. Raise it if a camera complains about a low battery while it starts.
